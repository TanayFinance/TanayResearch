The call

Starbucks runs over 38,000 stores across 86 markets with annual revenue above $36 billion. The thesis is that CEO Brian Niccol’s turnaround is gaining traction faster than the Street expects, and that the stock’s discount to peers closes as the results arrive.

Why STRONG BUY

  1. Turnaround inflection. The Green Apron service model, live in 2,000+ stores, has brought double-digit improvement in four-minute order fulfillment, and non-rewards customers posted their first year-over-year transaction growth since COVID.
  2. Margin upside. Menu simplification and labor efficiency support 200–300bps of margin expansion, versus 100–150bps expected by the Street.
  3. Valuation gap. SBUX trades at 20.5x forward earnings against a 24.8x peer median; a re-rating to 25–27x is justified as the turnaround proves out.
  4. China and international. New local leadership and delivery partnerships aim to stabilize China, the market most pressured by Luckin.

Valuation

A DCF (8.0% WACC, 3.0% terminal growth, 17.5% terminal operating margin) gives $80.31, and a multiples approach gives $114.50. Weighting them 35/65 yields $102.54; a 15% turnaround premium brings the target to $118.
